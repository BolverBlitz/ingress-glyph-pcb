Buttons & FeaturesSettings

A quick guide to the two buttons on your device and your personal drawing.

Mode control

Button 1 · press twice quickly

Switch between your chosen glyph sequence, random glyphs, and your Draw pages. The new display mode is saved after 10 seconds without button presses.

Flashlight

Button 1 · hold 2 seconds

Turn on the flashlight. Hold Button 1 for 2 seconds again to turn it off and return to your display.

Button 2 · press while flashlight is on

Switch flashlight brightness between 25%, 60%, and 100%.

Add a friend

Button 2 · hold on both devices

Bring the devices close together and hold Button 2 on each for at least 5 seconds. They pair as friends and exchange their personal drawings when available.

Bluetooth proximity must be enabled on both devices. You can also manage friends on the Nearby page.

Emergency signal

Both buttons · hold 10 seconds

Start a flashing white SOS signal and alert nearby devices.

Both buttons · hold 2 seconds again

Stop the SOS signal.

This is a local signal. It does not call emergency services.

Your personal Agent Glyph

One Draw page can act like your profile picture when devices exchange drawings.

  1. Set your Username on the Settings page and save it.
  2. On the Draw page, add or rename a page to exactly the same username.
  3. Draw your design, then tap Save on the Draw page.

Capital letters and spaces must match. For example, username Alex needs a Draw page named Alex, not alex. Without a matching saved page, there is no personal drawing to share. Friends can see it during pairing and in Friend Mode.
