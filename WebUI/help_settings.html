Settings help

Buttons & FeaturesSettings

What each option does, in plain language. Tap Save changes on the Settings page after editing.

Your identity

Username

The name nearby devices see. A saved Draw page with exactly the same name becomes your personal Agent Glyph.

Faction

Choose Resistance or Enlightened. This changes the app theme and the faction shown to nearby devices.

Time format

Choose 12-hour or 24-hour times in the web app. It does not change the controller's clock.

Board & display

Hardware revision

Choose the revision of your board. Rev 1 uses a 4.5 W LED safety cap. Rev 2 uses an 8 W cap and two board-temperature sensors. Changing this setting saves it and restarts the controller.

LED display rotation

Choose 0° or 180° so the LEDs look upright for the way your board is mounted. This rotates the output; it does not change your saved drawings.

Brightness

Adjusts the normal glyph and Draw display. Flashlight brightness has its own control.

Bluetooth & friends

Bluetooth proximity broadcasting

Lets this device announce itself and find nearby devices. Leave it on for the Nearby page, friend pairing, and local emergency alerts.

Friend Mode

When a saved friend is nearby and their Agent Glyph is available, show friends' drawings on the LEDs instead of the normal display. Friendship is checked once when a new visit is recorded, regardless of Friend Mode. Removing a friend on one board notifies the other board immediately when nearby; if it is offline, the removal is reconciled at the next recorded visit. The drawings cycle while those friends are in range.

Friendship requests expire after five minutes and disappear when the board restarts. Keep both boards nearby with Bluetooth enabled to accept a request.

LED power limits

Max LED power

A ceiling for LED power, not a target brightness. Dim patterns can use less. Rev 1 can be set up to 4.5 W; Rev 2 up to 8 W.

Minimum watts

Only prevents Max LED power from being set lower than this value. It does not force the LEDs to use that much power. Leave it at 0 W unless you need that setting constraint.

On Rev 2, the hotter board-temperature sensor reduces LED power above 50 °C and turns LEDs off at 70 °C. The LEDs also turn off if a sensor reading is unavailable. These safety limits always take priority over your Max LED power setting.

Wi-Fi & hotspot

Wi-Fi profiles

Save up to two networks. The controller tries WiFi 1 first, then WiFi 2. Use Scan nearby Wi-Fi to choose a network. Restart after saving a profile change to use it.

Fallback hotspot

If neither saved network connects, the controller starts its own Wi-Fi hotspot. Connect to it and open 192.168.4.1 in your browser. Set its name and password here; leave the password box empty to keep the existing password.

Status & maintenance

Device status

Shows live temperatures and estimated LED power. Hardware LED limit is the fixed ceiling for your board revision; Rev 2 temperature protection may lower the actual limit.

UTC time

The clock syncs from the internet when Wi-Fi is connected. If it has not synced, use Sync browser time so encounter times can be recorded.

Storage

Shows saved data and free space. You can export people and encounters before resetting that history.

Firmware update

Install a compatible firmware file here. Saved settings and Draw pages are kept; the controller restarts when the update finishes.
